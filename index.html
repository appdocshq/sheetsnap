The three things that make it different

Your columns are the fixed point

A set of columns per client, per property, per business — each with its own names and its own plain-English hints. Editing one never reshapes receipts you have already captured, so an old export still looks the way it did.

See where every number came from

Tap a field and the receipt dims to spotlight the exact place that value was read from. Anything the model was unsure of is underlined in amber, so you check the three that matter instead of re-reading all eight.

Works with no signal

Capture, on-device text recognition, typing values in by hand, the library, the filters and every export format work offline and signed out. A receipt captured with no signal is saved with its photo, gets its columns immediately so you can type into it, and is read for you automatically the next time you are online.

Pricing, plainly

Free: one set of columns and 15 receipts read a month, for good. No card, no expiry, no trial to forget to cancel — the free tier is the trial.

Pro ($7.99 a month or $49.99 a year): unlimited column sets, 200 receipts read a month, line items. Business ($119.99 a year): everything in Pro, 600 receipts read a month.

Photographing receipts, reading their text on your device, typing them in and exporting are unlimited on every plan, including free. Only having a receipt read for you counts against the monthly number, and a scan that fails is put back on your allowance.

Sheetsnap is for iPhone, iOS 17 and later.
